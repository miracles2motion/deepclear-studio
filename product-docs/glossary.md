# DeepClear Studio Glossary and Reference (v1.0)

## 1. Entertainment Industry, Film Production, and Clearance Terminology

| Term / Abbreviation | Full Expansion | Industry Definition | DeepClear Studio Operational Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **E&O Insurance** | Errors and Omissions Insurance | Specialized liability insurance relevant to film and television productions, covering certain claims involving matters such as copyright infringement, trademark issues, defamation, privacy, and title disputes. | DeepClear Studio does not provide or certify E&O insurance. It prepares an E&O Evidence Binder containing clearance findings, evidence references, modeled exposure ledgers, verification trails, and audit records for underwriting and clearance review. |
| **Chain of Title** | Chain of Title | The documented history establishing ownership, licensing rights, releases, and intellectual property provenance for a screenplay or motion picture. | DeepClear records screenplay versions, hazard identifiers, evidence references, and Clearance Gate outcomes in a tamper-evident SHA-256 state/history manifest to support auditability and continuity. This record does not itself establish legal ownership or chain of title. |
| **Script Clearance** | Screenplay Legal Clearance | The pre-production process of identifying and evaluating potentially sensitive brands, copyrighted works, real persons, locations, artwork, telephone numbers, domains, and other clearance hazards in a screenplay. | The primary workflow automated by DeepClear Studio, targeting whole-script hazard extraction in under 60 seconds for a 120-page screenplay, followed by evidence gathering, resolution proposals, and deterministic verification. |
| **Completion Bond** | Completion Guarantee / Bond | A financial contract issued by a third-party guarantor ensuring that a motion picture will be completed on schedule and within budget, or that investors are protected against specified completion risks. | Represented by the Bond Officer agent, which evaluates aggregate clearance posture and evidence readiness from a production-risk perspective and recommends release/readiness. The Bond Officer does not independently create VERIFIED status; the Clearance Gate remains the sole authority for verification. |
| **E&O Evidence Binder** | Errors & Omissions Evidence Binder | A structured evidence package used to organize clearance findings, rights information, verification records, and supporting documentation for underwriting or clearance review. | The client-side PDF evidence package generated by DeepClear Studio containing clearance findings, modeled exposure ledgers, verification trails, evidence references, policy headers, and relevant audit records. It is an evidence-preparation artifact and does not constitute insurer certification. |
| **Fountain** | Fountain Screenplay Syntax | An open-source, plain-text markup syntax that enables writers to craft formatted screenplays using clean, standard text conventions. | The native screenplay syntax ingested, parsed, and exported by DeepClear Studio, preserving standard sluglines, character cues, and parentheticals. |
| **Slugline** | Scene Heading | The standardized uppercase line starting a screenplay scene specifying interior/exterior, setting, and time of day (e.g., INT. TECH LAB - NIGHT). | DeepClear preserves screenplay location and production context during hazard analysis, mutation, and licensing workflows. Applicable production incentive calculations are evaluated independently from screenplay clearance. |
| **Parenthetical** | Wryly / Actor Direction Cue | A short directorial note placed between a character cue and spoken dialogue to indicate tone, physical action, or addressing party. | Parsed by the Script Supervisor to ensure character dialogue cues and delivery directions remain intact during prop mutations. |
| **Redline Diff** | Screenplay Redline Comparison | A side-by-side or visual comparison showing changes between screenplay versions. | DeepClear's visual inspection workspace, comparing the original screenplay against the proposed or verified production screenplay and highlighting applied clearance resolutions, substitutions, and other screenplay mutations. |
| **Prop Master** | Property Master | The film crew department head responsible for acquiring, manufacturing, and tracking physical props used by actors on camera. | The production role is treated as an industry stakeholder rather than a DeepClear agent. DeepClear may identify sensitive physical props and propose fictional alternatives or licensing routes for review. |
| **Sync License** | Synchronization License | An agreement granting the right to synchronize a copyrighted musical composition or master recording with moving visual images in a motion picture. | Evaluated by Legal Counsel as a potential resolution route for music-related hazards. Where licensing is unavailable or unsuitable, DeepClear may propose a fictional or original substitute. Both licensing and mutation routes remain subject to the same Clearance Gate evidence requirements. |
| **Safe 555 Exchange** | Safe Fictitious 555 Reserve | The 555-0100 through 555-0199 telephone number range reserved for entertainment and advertising use. | DeepClear may normalize an unvetted fictional telephone reference into the 555-0100 through 555-0199 range where appropriate, reducing the risk of inadvertently referencing a live subscriber number. |

## 2. Legal Doctrines, Intellectual Property, and Tort Law

| Term / Abbreviation | Full Expansion | Industry Legal Definition | DeepClear Studio Operational Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lanham Act Section 43(a)** | Title 15 U.S.C. Section 1125(a) | United States federal statute addressing claims including false designation of origin and false advertising, among related unfair-competition claims. | A legal framework used when evaluating trademark-related screenplay hazards. DeepClear may use the framework to inform its internal modeled risk exposure; the resulting dollar amount is not a prediction of actual legal liability. |
| **Title 17 U.S.C. Section 504** | Title 17 U.S.C. Section 504(c)(2) | United States Copyright Act provision governing remedies for infringement, establishing statutory damages up to $150,000 for willful infringement. | The statutory framework used to inform copyright-related modeled risk exposure for hazards involving unlicensed musical works, master recordings, poetry, fine art reproductions, and other copyrighted material. The resulting reserve is an internal modeling aid, not a prediction of actual damages. |
| **Cal. Civ. Code Section 3344** | California Civil Code Section 3344 | California statute addressing unauthorized commercial use of another person's name, voice, signature, photograph, or likeness. | A legal framework surfaced for People/Personality hazards involving potentially identifiable living individuals. DeepClear uses the framework to inform internal modeled risk exposure and clearance review. |
| **Rogers v. Grimaldi** | Rogers v. Grimaldi, 875 F.2d 994 (2d Cir. 1989) | Landmark Second Circuit legal precedent establishing that the Lanham Act does not apply to artistic works unless the mark has no artistic relevance or explicitly misleads consumers. | A legal framework surfaced during agent deliberation when evaluating expressive uses of trademarks in artistic works and determining whether a reference may warrant further legal review or an alternative resolution. |
| **Fair Use** | 17 U.S.C. Section 107 Fair Use Doctrine | Statutory exception to copyright owner exclusivity, permitting limited unlicensed use for purposes such as criticism, comment, parody, scholarship, or artistic transformation. | Evaluated by Legal Counsel and The Director as one factor in determining whether a copyrighted reference may warrant further legal review, retention, licensing, or fictional substitution. DeepClear does not make a definitive legal determination of fair use. |
| **FAA Part 107** | 14 CFR Part 107 Drone Regulations | Federal Aviation Administration regulations governing commercial operations of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) over public areas and critical infrastructure. | Used as a regulatory reference when evaluating screenplay scenarios involving drone or aerial cinematography requirements. Any associated financial value is treated as internal modeled risk exposure rather than actual statutory liability. |
| **ACPA** | Anticybersquatting Consumer Protection Act | Federal statute (15 U.S.C. Section 1125(d)) imposing statutory damages between $1,000 and $100,000 for bad-faith registration or use of domain names identical or confusingly similar to distinctive marks. | Applied when evaluating screenplay references to potentially conflicting domain names. DeepClear may flag domain collisions and model associated exposure; it does not represent all fictional URLs as automatically resolving to studio-owned domains. |
| **Nice Classification** | International Classification of Goods & Services | International trademark classification system (Classes 1 through 45) administered by WIPO and USPTO to categorize goods and services for trademark registration. | Surfaced in the Parallel Inspector Drawer when available to show the commercial classification associated with a trademark search result. Classification helps describe the goods or services involved but does not itself establish trademark clearance. |
| **Clearance Gate / Gate-Passed State** | Deterministic Clearance Verification State | A product-level verification state indicating that configured evidence and rule conditions have been satisfied for a particular clearance decision. | DeepClear's deterministic control layer permits a hazard to transition to VERIFIED only when required evidence is present, active conflicts are cleared, and configured ruleset conditions pass. The Gate-Passed State is an internal product verification state and does not constitute legal safe harbor, insurance certification, or a guarantee of zero liability. |

## 3. Tax Credits, Incentives, and Economic Production Financing

| Term / Abbreviation | Full Expansion | Industry Financial Definition | DeepClear Studio Operational Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **QPE** | Qualified Production Expenditure | Eligible production spending that may qualify for applicable statutory film or television production incentives. | The expenditure input used by DeepClear's incentive estimation workflow. Illustrative estimated incentive eligibility is calculated as QPE multiplied by an applicable illustrative rate; eligibility is not guaranteed and is evaluated independently from modeled clearance risk. |
| **Transferable Tax Credit** | Transferable Production Tax Credit | A film tax incentive that a production company can sell to third-party corporate entities for immediate production cash. | Used as a conceptual production-financing mechanism in applicable jurisdictions such as Georgia. DeepClear models applicable incentive eligibility independently from clearance risk and does not represent the estimated credit as guaranteed production cash. |
| **Refundable Tax Credit** | Refundable Production Tax Credit | A tax incentive that may allow an eligible production to receive a refund or payment for qualifying credit amounts under applicable state rules. | Referenced in DeepClear's incentive modeling for jurisdictions such as New Mexico. DeepClear uses applicable program rates to estimate potential incentive eligibility; the estimate is illustrative and does not represent guaranteed cash financing. |
| **Non-Transferable Credit** | Non-Transferable Production Tax Credit | A tax credit that can only offset direct corporate tax liability within the issuing state, requiring independent CPA expenditure audits and specialized allocation. | Modeled in DeepClear for applicable state incentive frameworks, including California's Film and Television Tax Credit Program 4.0. The model uses applicable program rates as illustrative inputs and does not guarantee eligibility. |
| **Strict Independence Principle** | Strict Financial Independence Principle | Entertainment underwriting rule dictating that modeled clearance risk and production tax incentives must be evaluated independently and never netted together. | Principle that modeled clearance risk and production incentive eligibility must be evaluated independently and never netted together. |

## 4. DeepClear Studio Swarm Architecture and Agent Dynamics

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Script Supervisor** | `script_supervisor` | Continuity-obsessed, brisk, precise (Pitch: 1.12, Rate: 1.04). | Manages screenplay formatting, scene sluglines, dialogue cues, and executes regular expression stutter defense against duplicated words. |
| **Studio Legal Counsel** | `legal_counsel` | Risk-averse, analytical, statutory authority (Pitch: 1.08, Rate: 1.02). | Evaluates trademark, copyright, and related legal hazards, constructs targeted Parallel search queries, interprets retrieved evidence, and proposes or evaluates resolution routes. It cannot independently bypass or override the Clearance Gate. |
| **Location Manager** | `location_manager` | Budget-conscious, logistical, practical (Pitch: 0.96, Rate: 1.02). | Manages screenplay location and production-facility hazards, evaluates relevant permitting and location evidence, and supports location-related licensing or mutation routes. Applicable incentive calculations are evaluated independently from clearance verification. |
| **The Director** | `director` | Cinematic, visionary, artistically protective (Pitch: 0.92, Rate: 1.02). | Champions dramatic stakes, narrative authenticity, and creative intent during agent deliberation. May argue for retaining expressive references or propose creative alternatives, while remaining subject to evidence requirements and the Clearance Gate. |
| **Bond Officer** | `bond_officer` | Deliberate, institutional, production-risk focused (Pitch: 0.88, Rate: 0.98). | Evaluates aggregate clearance posture, evidence readiness, and production-risk considerations, and recommends release/readiness. The Bond Officer does not hold ultimate verification authority and cannot independently create VERIFIED status or bypass the Clearance Gate. |
| **Dialectic Debate** | Dialectic Debate Protocol | — | Structured argumentative exchange between legal, creative, production, continuity, and underwriting-oriented perspectives used to evaluate screenplay hazards and negotiate potential resolution routes. |
| **Bounded Debate** | 3-Turn Loop Ceiling | An architectural guardrail limiting agent debate turns to a maximum of 3 turns per hazard to prevent infinite reasoning loops and unnecessary token consumption. | Limits debate arguments to 3 turns per hazard before escalating unresolved items to HUMAN_REVIEW. |
| **HUMAN_REVIEW** | Controlled Human Review State | A controlled escalation state used when automated reasoning cannot establish an acceptable resolution within configured constraints or human judgment is required. | Provides a controlled human-resolution path. | **COUNSEL_RESOLUTION** | Counsel Resolution Step | A recorded human legal-resolution step following controlled escalation. | Records the human resolution decision and returns the hazard to evidence evaluation. It cannot directly create VERIFIED status. |
| **Stutter Defense** | Screenplay Mutation Stutter Defense | Automated regular expression filters that sanitize text substitutions against duplicated adjacent words and clashing indefinite articles. | Runs automatically in mutateScriptText: eliminates double words (e.g., 'vintage vintage' -> 'vintage') and article collisions (e.g., 'An an' -> 'An'). |
| **Agent Mentions** | Multi-Agent Color Tagging (@agent) | Interactive chat feature allowing producers to address specific crew members (e.g., @legal_counsel, @director) using autocomplete popovers. | Triggers targeted conversational advisory responses from the selected agent profile without directly mutating screenplay text, changing liability ledgers, or bypassing the Clearance Gate. |
| **License Route** | Licensing Resolution Route | A resolution path in which a screenplay hazard is addressed through obtaining or documenting applicable rights rather than substituting the referenced material. | Route 'license' leaves original screenplay text unaltered while attaching verified rights/permit evidence; subject to the same Clearance Gate verification requirements as mutations. |

## 5. Deterministic Verification, Telemetry, and Cryptographic Security

| Term / Abbreviation | Full Expansion | Technical & Security Definition | DeepClear Studio Operational Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clearance Gate** | Deterministic Clearance Gate | A programmatic control layer that evaluates whether required evidence, conflict conditions, and configured ruleset requirements are satisfied before permitting a verification state transition. | The sole authority for transitioning a hazard to VERIFIED. No AI agent, UI action, screenplay mutation, licensing route, or client-side state change can bypass the Gate's verification conditions. |
| **Fail-Closed Policy** | Fail-Closed Architecture | A security and risk-management design pattern in which missing, invalid, ambiguous, or failed verification evidence does not produce an affirmative verified state. | If external evidence retrieval fails, times out, returns unresolved conflicts, or otherwise fails configured verification requirements, DeepClear does not mark the hazard VERIFIED and routes it according to the configured escalation path. |
| **parallelData** | Parallel Evidence / Telemetry Payload | Structured data captured from an external Parallel Web Systems search and normalized by DeepClear for evidence evaluation. | Exposed through the Parallel Inspector Drawer and used to record evidence metadata such as search identifiers, latency, registry status, classification information, and other fields required by DeepClear's normalized evidence contract. |
| **verified: true** | DeepClear Verification Status | A boolean field in DeepClear's normalized evidence contract indicating that the evidence adapter has determined the verification condition required by the configured Clearance Gate is satisfied. | Used as one of the mandatory evidence conditions evaluated by the Clearance Gate. External search systems provide underlying evidence; DeepClear normalizes and evaluates that evidence before permitting a VERIFIED transition. |
| **Active Conflicts** | Active Clearance Conflicts | The number of unresolved conflicts represented in DeepClear's normalized hazard and evidence state. | A configured Clearance Gate condition requiring activeConflicts === 0 before VERIFIED status is permitted. Conflicts are normalized by DeepClear from relevant evidence rather than treated as a raw registry field. |
| **Inspector Drawer** | Parallel Inspector Drawer | A slide-out telemetry drawer in the studio dashboard displaying raw query parameters, search IDs, latencies, and extracted snippets. | Provides judges, attorneys, and underwriters with full transparency into live external search lookups, response times, and trademark classifications. |
| **Clearance Passport** | Cryptographic Clearance Passport | A tamper-evident audit record associated with a screenplay version and its clearance history. | Records screenplay version information, hazard identifiers, evidence references, and Clearance Gate outcomes. DeepClear generates a SHA-256-based state/history manifest to support tamper detection, auditability, and continuity across screenplay revisions. |
| **SHA-256 State/History Manifest** | SHA-256 State/History Manifest | A cryptographic representation of recorded application state and clearance history used to detect unauthorized changes to the recorded record. | Generated as part of the Clearance Passport to represent screenplay version information, hazard history, evidence references, and Clearance Gate outcomes, supporting tamper detection and continuity across revisions. |
| **In-Memory Cache** | 30-Minute Query Cache (`getCachedParallelQuery`) | A fast in-memory key-value cache that stores normalized search queries and verification results for 30 minutes during active sessions. | Reduces redundant external evidence searches when identical or equivalent queries recur during an active session, helping conserve API usage and reduce unnecessary network requests. |
| **Model Cascade** | Automatic Gemini Model Cascade (`MODEL_CANDIDATES`) | A self-healing model invoker that automatically cascades through 7 prioritized Gemini Flash and Pro models if a specific endpoint experiences high demand. | Provides automatic fallback across 7 configured Gemini models when the preferred model is unavailable, deprecated, or unable to serve the request. |
| **Dynamic Discovery** | Dynamic Model Registry Discovery | An automated fallback mechanism that queries Google's Generative Language ModelService directly if all local candidate models return deprecation errors. | Queries `generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models` dynamically to discover live compatible Gemini model IDs. |
| **Modeled Risk Exposure** | Internal Modeled Risk Exposure | An internal illustrative estimate representing potential exposure associated with identified screenplay hazards. | Used by DeepClear for workflow prioritization, risk visualization, and evidence preparation. Modeled Risk Exposure is not a prediction of actual legal liability, damages, insurance coverage, settlement value, or final adjudicated exposure. |
| **Normalized Evidence Contract** | DeepClear Normalized Evidence Contract | A structured internal representation of external verification evidence used by application logic. | Contains evidence fields such as verification status, source, search identifier, latency, registry status, applicable classification, active conflicts, and ruleset satisfaction. The Clearance Gate evaluates this normalized contract rather than relying directly on raw external responses. |
